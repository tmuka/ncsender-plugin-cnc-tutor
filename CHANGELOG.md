# Changelog

## 0.2.1: Better pictures

- Feeds and speeds: a new picture of the chip at the same RPM with the feed too slow (dust, heat, burn marks), just right (curled chips) and too fast (thick chunks, chatter). The lesson adds that feeds and speeds go with one bit in one material: work them out again when either changes.
- Changing a bit: a new picture of how far the bit goes into the collet: too far out, just right, and too far in with the flutes inside the collet.
- Holding the work: a side view of the spindle crashing into a clamp taller than the stock, and vacuum clamps as a production timesaver.
- Depth of cut and stepover: a longer bit, so the collet no longer plunges into the stock.
- Work coordinates: many people happily use G54 for every project, and what power users keep the others for.
- Climb vs conventional: also called left and right cut (G41 and G42).
- Tool changes: the magazine list lines up when tool numbers have one and two digits.
- The sidebar scrolls to the lesson you're on, and the tutor reopens on the student's last lesson on any screen.

## 0.2.0: Students, probing and tools

- Level 6, Probing and tool changes: changing a bit and the collet, how a probe works, the touch plate, the tool length setter, tool changes and the ATC, and the 3D probe. Each shows what your machine has set up in ncSender; a lesson for something it doesn’t have is marked optional and says so. Try it watches the probe input, a probe cycle, a TLS measurement and a tool change.
- Lessons, quizzes, pictures and the practice program follow ncSender’s units: inch machines get inch examples and an inch practice program.
- Students, for a shared machine, one at a time: Who’s learning? when the tutor opens, each student’s own progress, Switch student and Sign out in the sidebar, and “Still you?” after a 2-hour break. Add, rename and remove students; only a name and a color are kept.
- Guest: try the lessons without saving progress.
- Class progress: every student’s lessons per level, Try it steps seen, first-try quiz score and last active.
- Your progress from 0.1 becomes Student 1 (rename it).

## 0.1.1: Animated pictures

- Every lesson picture redrawn, and the ones that show motion are animated: the gantry moving X, Y and Z, homing to your home corner, jog steps, lifting before moving across, the work offset, zeroing X/Y and Z, a longer bit cutting deeper, the air cut, spinning bits throwing chips up or down, a bit cutting its chip, depth and stepover passes, climb around a part, and a profile with tabs.
- Tap a picture to pause it. With reduced motion turned on, each shows a still frame.

## 0.1.0: First lessons

- 19 lessons in five levels, from safety and X Y Z to feeds and speeds, plus Your first part.
- Try it: the tutor shrinks to a card and watches the machine while you jog, home, zero, load and run.
- Your machine, Where am I? (with a map of the table) and a Glossary.
- A chip load calculator with QuickCut's starting feeds.
- Links to ncSender's docs from every lesson.
- Read only: it never sends anything to the controller.
